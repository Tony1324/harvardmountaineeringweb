About

Since 1924, HMC has been the home for climbers and mountaineers of Harvard. We are dedicated to facilitating access to the outdoors for all.

Gear

As a member of HMC, you will have access free of charge to our entire gear collection during club trips and your own adventures, which includes climbing ropes, harnesses, helmets, ice axes, crampons, mountaineering boots, sleeping bags, tents, and more.

To borrow gear, please visit our gear hours after the general meetings, or reach out to the Gear Tsars.
